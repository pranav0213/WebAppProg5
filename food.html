Which row breaks first?

As the browser window is narrowed, the foods row (col-md) breaks first, stacking vertically at the medium breakpoint (768px). The letters row (col-sm-2) stays horizontal longer and doesn't break until the small breakpoint (576px). This is because col-md columns only stay side-by-side at md width and up, while col-sm-2 columns stay side-by-side at the lower sm width and up, so col-md collapses to full width at a wider screen size than col-sm-2 does.
